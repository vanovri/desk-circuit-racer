SOURCE AND SCALE

An annotated example layout.

The scene starts from supplied synthetic top-down boxes. It is not a reconstruction of your desk photograph. Dimensions use nominal centimeters for consistent arcade motion and have not been calibrated against a photograph.

Physics and demo evidence

Motion runs at 120 fixed simulation steps per second, separately from display refresh. Collision checks sweep the car footprint between old and new positions. Six gates must be reached in order to finish a lap. The guided demo uses steering, throttle and braking through the same model, at 4× simulation speed. It does not teleport the car or represent an AI model call.

Loss of focus pauses the run and clears held controls. The clock measures active simulated time. This arcade model permits stationary turning and uses conservative axis-aligned obstacle envelopes. It is not a calibrated model of an RC car.
